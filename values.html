What We Stand For

Our values.

The principles that shape every case we open, and every one we choose not to.

Child-First

Safety before speed

Every action is weighed against what actually keeps a child safer — not what closes a case fastest.

Evidence-Bound

Facts, not assumptions

We only refer what we can substantiate. Weak leads stay open, not padded into a case file.

In Our Lane

Investigate, don't intervene

We build cases. Law enforcement makes arrests. We're careful never to blur that line.

Accountable

Answerable to someone

We're building the formal oversight our work deserves, not asking for trust without it.

In Practice

Values only matter under pressure.

High-volume intake means the temptation to move fast is constant. We built our review process — not our headlines — around these four principles, because that's where they actually get tested: a borderline lead, an ambiguous domain, a tip that arrives at 3am. The bar stays the same either way.
